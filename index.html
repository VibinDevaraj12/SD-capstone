Simplified DC calculation

Input powerPin = Vin × Iin
Output powerPout = Vout × Iout
Power lossPloss = Pin − Pout
Efficiencyη = (Pout / Pin) × 100%
Voltage dropΔV = Vin − Vout
Reverse currentIrev(A) = Irev(mA) / 1000
ⓘ

For AC input, measure real power. Highly rippled rectifier waveforms may require averaging instantaneous power, v(t) × i(t), rather than multiplying average voltage and current.

A result outside the passive-rectifier range indicates a measurement/model inconsistency to investigate—not, by itself, a verified hardware fault.
